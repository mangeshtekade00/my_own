Your responsibility:
Educate students about genuine documentation.
Check consistency in their resumes and applications.
Help them understand employment gaps.
Explain how companies verify previous employment.
Help resolve genuine discrepancies.
Important: Never encourage fake experience, forged salary slips or fabricated employment records. Obtain consent and protect student documents.

6. Your job guarantee model
Since your proposed model is ₹1.5 lakh per student, with at least 50 students per batch and 100 days of training, you need a written placement policy.
I recommend defining:
Minimum attendance.
Mandatory assignment completion.
Technical assessment criteria.
Mock interview participation.
Genuine documentation requirements.
Number of placement opportunities.
Eligibility conditions.
Refund or alternative-placement provisions.
Do not advertise an unconditional job guarantee unless you can contractually and financially honour it.
